 Algebraic Operations: Inverses, commutativity checks, idempotent reductions, and algebraic closure under valid actions.
 
	Inverses: $\text{Push}(x) \circ \text{Pop}() = \text{id}$ (enables undo/backtracking).
	
	Commutativity: If $\text{Op}_A$ and $\text{Op}_B$ access disjoint memory/state, $\text{Op}_A \circ \text{Op}_B = \text{Op}_B \circ \text{Op}_A$ (enables automatic parallelization).
	
	Idempotence: $\text{MarkVisited}(v) \circ \text{MarkVisited}(v) = \text{MarkVisited}(v)$ (enables redundant step elimination).
 
 Focus: Defines the grammar of atomic actions that can manipulate domain states safely.
